# Vision

## Who this is for

IncidentLens is intended for security responders, site reliability engineers,
platform engineers, and incident leads who need a shared view of a developing
incident.

## Intended outcomes

Make an investigation easier to follow: what happened, what evidence supports
that statement, what remains uncertain, and what should be checked next.

The proposed experience favors evidence-linked timelines, explicit hypotheses,
and reviewable handoffs over opaque verdicts. A security concern and an
availability concern may share evidence without having the same root cause.

## Non-goals

- Autonomous containment, remediation, or changes to production systems.
- Claims of complete detection coverage or guaranteed root-cause identification.
- Replacing existing observability, security, or incident-management tools.
- Publishing proprietary investigation implementation.

## Questions to validate

Which evidence references are most useful during a cross-team handoff? How should
responders record conflicting observations? What makes uncertainty actionable
without overstating confidence?

Answers should come from workflow feedback using synthetic or explicitly
authorized, sanitized material, not from collecting sensitive data in public
issues.
