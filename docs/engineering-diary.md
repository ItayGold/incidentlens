# Engineering diary

## 2026-10-07: Establishing a public boundary

Prepared the public foundation for IncidentLens as a concept-stage project
covering both security and operational incident investigation.

Decisions:

- Keep the initial scaffold dependency-free and runnable as a local static page.
- Describe intended capabilities separately from what is implemented.
- Use fictional evidence and explicitly unverified hypotheses in the example.
- Publish high-level boundaries, not collectors, mappings, taxonomy, algorithms,
  prompts, scoring, or reasoning implementation.
- Avoid implying that private components, integrations, or trust controls exist.
- Leave licensing undecided rather than choosing reuse permissions for the owner.

The next engineering decision is the reviewable output contract, informed by
workflow feedback. No ingestion or analysis service was implemented in this entry.
